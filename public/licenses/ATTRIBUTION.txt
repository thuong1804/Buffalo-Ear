CHANGES MADE FOR BUFFALO EAR
- Selected a compact 3,000-entry learning subset with Vietnamese meanings.
- Retained the app's original 507 entries and their meanings.
- Selected dictionary senses using the study list's part of speech where available.
- Added editorial corrections for ambiguous meanings and missing pronunciations.
- Normalized IPA into the 44-sound teaching inventory, omitting stress marks,
  normalizing syllabic consonants and /i, u/ into the available teaching symbols.
- Added explicit editorial levels for legacy and supplemental everyday items.
- Stored rows as [word, ipa, meaning, level] for efficient local use.

Pinned source revisions and hashes are in data/sources.json in the source project.
Build steps and corrections are in scripts/build-vocabulary.py.
Adapted dataset: data/vocabulary.json, CC BY-SA 4.0.

This is a reference study dataset, not an official Oxford 3000 release.
Levels apply to headwords as study guidance; senses may differ in level.
